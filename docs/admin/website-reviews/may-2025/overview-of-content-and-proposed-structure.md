# Overview of Dinis Cruz’s Research Content (docs.diniscruz.ai)

**Welcome!** This document provides a structured overview of all content published on **Dinis Cruz’s research site**. It is organized by topic to help readers (and even language models) easily discover the scope of work — from cybersecurity and knowledge graphs to the future of news, projects, and more. Each entry below includes a brief description and a direct link to the full post or resource. Use this as a guide to navigate the site and explore Dinis’s research, ideas, and presentations.

## Future of News

Insights into how journalism and news media can evolve — improving trust, provenance, and monetization — with the help of AI, micropayments, and identity graphs.

* **Monetising Trust and Knowledge: How News Providers can leverage Personalised Semantic Graphs** – Explores using personalized knowledge graphs for readers, enabling news organizations to build trust and new value streams by curating content specific to each reader’s context [(Read)](http://docs.diniscruz.ai/2025/02/02/monetising-trust-and-knowledge-for-news-providers.html).
* **Journalists’ Challenges with Digital Content Provenance and Trust** – Examines the difficulties journalists face in verifying digital content in the age of deepfakes and AI, and suggests solutions (like content provenance frameworks and graph-based traceability) to bolster trust in media [(Read)](http://docs.diniscruz.ai/2025/03/24/journalists-challenges-with-digital-content-provenance-and-trust.html).
* **The Future of News: Building Trust Through Fact Provenance** – Discusses establishing “webs of trust” around news by attaching verifiable provenance data to facts, sources, and quotes, thereby helping readers distinguish trustworthy information [(Read)](http://docs.diniscruz.ai/2025/02/05/the-future-of-news-building-trust-through-fact-provenance.html).
* **The Future of News Monetization: Embracing Micro and Nano Payments** – Argues that news media should adopt micro-payment and nano-payment models (e.g. pay-per-article or per-second) to sustain journalism, describing how these tiny transactions could be implemented and why they’re needed [(Read)](http://docs.diniscruz.ai/2025/04/02/the-future-of-news-monetization__embracing-micro-and-nano-payments.html).
* **Strengthening Trust in News: Implementing Identity Graphs for Authors and Sources** – Proposes using “identity graphs” that link journalists, sources, and content to their digital reputations, so that readers can quickly assess the credibility of an author or source through a transparent trust network [(Read)](http://docs.diniscruz.ai/2025/04/21/strengthening-trust-in-news__implementing-identity-graphs-for-authors-and-sources.html).
